How to read the confusion matrices

Rows = true class, columns = predicted class, normalized per row; the diagonal is correct predictions. The weak models (Basic CNN, ResNet18) are nearly blind on this fine-grained 34-class problem; MobileNetV2 learns a handful of classes; DINOv2 linear probing covers the diagonal broadly — which is why it dominates the ensemble. Full per-class tables and top-confusion lists live in plantwild_report.md and model/plantwild_ensemble_results.json.
